# Known Limitations

- Browser, Docker, Turso staging, Supabase staging, email delivery, and live AI validation require environment-specific credentials/capabilities and are not assumed by local checks.
- Generated CVs, letters, essays, decks, and interview feedback are drafts; review facts, dates, claims, and formatting before sharing.
- Rate limits are process-local unless a shared adapter is configured.
- Some legacy screens still need localization and dark-mode cleanup.
